## Phase A conclusions

1. The main root cause of “what to say” errors is insight selection/binding, especially raw production primary versus normalized evidence.
2. Weak delivery is appropriate for low-information color echoes/unified palettes; omit when facts are sparse or unknown.
3. The projection layer should convert controlled evidence (not deterministic copy) into a compact, user-facing semantic point.
4. Prompt freeze, schema freeze, and benchmark separation remain unchanged.
5. These findings are sufficient to make the Phase B decision on the independent presentation harness.
